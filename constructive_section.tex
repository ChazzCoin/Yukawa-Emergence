\subsection{The object to be fixed}\label{sec:object}
% Manager decision 2026-10-23 (files/R1-constructive-spec.md).
The no-go of Secs.~\ref{sec:obstruction}--\ref{sec:external} is the $\varepsilon\to0$ limit
of a non-degenerate problem. Write the data-fed neutrino Hermitian as
\begin{equation}\label{eq:epsilon-split}
  \Hnu \;=\; \Hnu^{(0)} + \varepsilon\,K,\qquad
  \Hnu^{(0)}\big|_{E}=\lambda\,\one_E ,
\end{equation}
where $E$ is the atmospheric ($\nu_2$--$\nu_3$) pair and $K|_E$ is the degeneracy-lifting
term. For $\varepsilon\neq0$ the rotation $R(\theta)$ inside $E$ is no longer a symmetry of
$\Hnu$. The frame is then physical, and the orientation that has to be supplied is the
\emph{eigenframe of $K|_E$}, not $\theta_{23}$ taken alone.

\subsection{Conditions on a candidate operator}\label{sec:conditions}
A candidate external operator $Q$ \emph{passes} only if it meets all five conditions:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] \textbf{Non-inert:} $Q|_E\not\propto\one_E$.
\item[(b)] \textbf{Externally oriented:} the eigen-orientation of $Q$ on $E$ is fixed by data other
  than the mixing angles.
\item[(c)] \textbf{Fixes the frame:} $Q$ fixes the eigenframe of $K|_E$ in \eqref{eq:epsilon-split}.
\item[(d)] \textbf{Beats the null:} the selected orientation beats a null ensemble of generic
  tilted $(\He,\Hnu)$ configurations, so it is not a look-elsewhere coincidence.
\item[(e)] \textbf{Scorable:} it yields a number, a sign or an octant that can be scored against
  NuFIT~6.0 \cite{nufit6} without using $\theta_{23}$ as an input, with the variant (IC19 without
  SK-atm, or IC24 with SK-atm) named.
\end{enumerate}
Conditions (a) and (b) are those of Cor.~\ref{cor:external}; (c)--(e) make them testable.
"Fails" is a complete verdict when its reason is given.

\subsection{D1: scale (renormalization-group running)}\label{sec:D1}

from exact $\mu$--$\tau$ degeneracy, generates $K|_E$ through the charged-lepton Yukawa

\emph{Gate.} Reproduce numerically the analytic running of the mixing angles of

Zhou~\cite{zhou2014}; review: Xing and Zhao~\cite{xingzhao2016}.\\

\emph{Setup.} The record runs at one loop, below the lightest seesaw scale $M_1$, from a
$\mu$--$\tau$ permutation-symmetric seed at $M_1$ down to $M_Z$, in the SM and in the MSSM. Before
any novel number it passes five gates, including the analytic angle RGEs of

\emph{Result.}
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Sign.} $\operatorname{sign}(\theta_{23}(M_Z)-\pi/4)=+\operatorname{sign}(C)\,
  \operatorname{sign}(\Delta m^2_{\rm atm})$, with $C=-3/2$ (SM) and $C=+1$ (MSSM). For normal
  ordering the SM gives the lower octant and the MSSM the upper. The octant therefore follows the

\item \emph{Which pair is oriented.} The running rotates the solar pair far more than the

  $\mu$--$\tau$ seed (Sec.~D1 of the verifier). It holds for inverted ordering at every
  $m_{\rm lightest}$. For normal ordering it holds above $m_{\rm lightest}\simeq4.4$~meV (both

  but there the running is smallest.
\item \emph{Seed consistency (not a D1 result).} The permutation seed has $\theta_{13}=0$; running
  generates at most $1.78^\circ$ under $\Lambda$CDM, against the measured $8.5^\circ$.

\emph{Verdict on (a)--(e).}
\begin{itemize}
\item (a) \textbf{Passes:} the running term on $E$ is rank one, not $\propto\one_E$.
\item (b) \textbf{Passes in part:} its direction $e_\tau$ is set by the charged-lepton mass

\item (c) \textbf{Fails:} the running does not single out the $\nu_2$--$\nu_3$ frame; it orients the

\item (d) \textbf{Unscored:} the record's null is unmatched, tests $\theta_{23}$ only, and uses

  As a closeness figure only (not a (d) score): at the one allowed configuration that reaches the

\item (e) \textbf{Fails}, except in the tuned corner above, and there $\theta_{13}$ is wrong.

\textbf{D1 fails as a data-fixed $\theta_{23}$ mechanism.}

\emph{Setup.} The gate reproduces Grimus--Lavoura and Harrison--Scott: $\mu$--$\tau$ reflection on
a complex-symmetric $m_\nu$ gives $|U_{\mu j}|=|U_{\tau j}|$ and $\theta_{23}=45^\circ$ exactly,

\emph{Result: what $m_\nu$ carries at $\varepsilon\to0$} (rigorous, checked against a closed form).
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Frame freedom.} $\Hnu$ leaves a $u(2)$ frame freedom on the degenerate $\nu_2$--$\nu_3$
  pair (4 real dimensions in the block). $m_\nu$ cuts it to $o(2)$ (1 dimension). The underlying
  fact is known: for exactly degenerate Majorana neutrinos the mixing matrix can be rotated away
  only if all have the same CP parity, and cannot be when one has the opposite parity
  (Branco, Rebelo and Silva-Marcos~\cite{brsm1999}, pp.~2--4, shown there for three degenerate
  neutrinos). The pair statement is the lab's $2\times2$ sub-case. It holds only in the degenerate
  limit, which the cosmological bounds exclude (Sec.~\ref{sec:object}).
\item \emph{Stationarity.} Along the remaining direction
  $d\theta_{23}/d\varphi=0.8414\cos(\psi/2)$, with $\psi$ the relative Majorana phase. So
  $\theta_{23}$ is stationary to first order at opposite CP parity ($\psi=\pi$).
\item \emph{Under the reflection.} With opposite $\nu_2$--$\nu_3$ parity, $\theta_{23}$ stays
  exactly $45^\circ$ along the remaining direction while $\theta_{13}$ runs over
  $0.067^\circ$--$34.6^\circ$. With the same parity, the reflection fixes the frame discretely.
\item \emph{Solar pair.} It has the same structure: $d\theta_{12}/d\varphi=1$ at $\psi=0$ and $0$ at
  $\psi=\pi$.
\end{itemize}
\emph{Result: the measured deviation.}
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Size.} Reaching the NuFIT~6.0 central values takes a reflection-odd breaking of relative size
  $\rho\approx0.02$--$0.13$ over the $\Lambda$CDM-allowed $\Sigma m_\nu$.
\item \emph{Null.} A matched-norm random odd breaking lands in the measured octant at chance:
  $P=0.497$--$0.509$ in all 12 variant$\times m_{\rm lightest}$ cells.
\item \emph{Data-fixed direction.} The only odd direction fixed by data is the $\tau$-Yukawa
  splitting $m\to EmE$. It sets the octant by
  $\operatorname{sign}(\eta)\operatorname{sign}(\Delta m^2_{\rm atm})$, with $\eta=J_\tau$, D1's
  running strength; the T1 and T2 sign rules agree. In the SM the sign is data-fixed, but
  $|J_\tau|=2.8\times10^{-5}$ is 653--3027 times too small. In the MSSM the sign is a model input:
  $\tan\beta=50$ reaches 2 of 24 normal-ordering IC19 (without SK-atm) rows, and it has the wrong
  sign for normal-ordering IC24 (with SK-atm) and for inverted ordering.
\item \emph{Inverted ordering.} Reaching the data $\theta_{23}$ inflates
  $\Delta m^2_{21}/|\Delta m^2_{\rm atm}|$ by a factor of 2.9--23.6.
\end{itemize}
\emph{Verdict on (a)--(e).}
\begin{itemize}
\item (a) \textbf{Passes:} $m_\nu$ is non-inert on the pair, reducing its frame freedom from 4
  dimensions to 1.
\item (b) \textbf{Fails:} $45^\circ$ and $\pm90^\circ$ come from the stated reflection input, not
  from data. The breaking that the measured deviation needs has a free orientation. So (b) holds
  only under an imposed symmetry, and only for the symmetric values.
\item (c) \textbf{Fails:} no datum orients the remaining breaking.
\item (d) \textbf{Not applicable:} there is no octant prediction to test. The chance level
  $P=0.5$ against the matched-norm null is forced by the reflection symmetry.
\item (e) \textbf{Not scored:} the reflection gives $\delta=\pm90^\circ$ with a free sign. Against

  variant, as an input, not as a prediction.
\end{itemize}
\textbf{D2 fails as a data-fixed $\theta_{23}$ mechanism.} It supplies the honest content of the
\emph{erased dimension}: $m_\nu$ reduces the degenerate-pair freedom from 4 to 1 and makes $45^\circ$
stationary at opposite CP parity, but no datum orients the breaking that remains.

\emph{Structure (exact).}
\begin{remark}[Flavor labels are functions of $\He$]\label{rem:labels}
Let the charged-lepton masses $a_\alpha=m_\alpha^2$ be distinct. Every operator diagonal in the
charged-lepton basis is then a polynomial in $\He$ of degree at most 2, by Lagrange interpolation on
the three eigenvalues. In particular a harmonic-label operator
$\hat\Phi=\operatorname{diag}(n_e,n_\mu,n_\tau)$ on flavor states equals $f(\He)$ with
$f(a_\alpha)=n_\alpha$; the unitary form $\operatorname{diag}(e^{2\pi i n_\alpha/N})$ is a polynomial
with complex coefficients. So every functional of $(\hat\Phi,\Hnu)$ is a functional of
$(\He,\Hnu)$, and the harmonic structure ($N$, $V_m$, gcd/LCM) enters only through three numbers.
If the labels sit on mass states instead, $\hat\Phi=q(\Hnu)$ commutes with $\Hnu$ and is inert by
construction.
\end{remark}
\noindent Consequences, stated for functionals \emph{restricted to $E$}:
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Exact $\mu$--$\tau$ plane (the paper's setting; $E$ invariant under $\He$).}
  $f(\He)|_E=c_f\one+\tfrac12 d_f\sigma_z$ with $d_f=f(m_\tau^2)-f(m_\mu^2)$, so the eigenframe
  is the flavor frame for every $f$ with $d_f\neq0$. Then
  $\Tr(f(\He)\Hnu(\theta))=\mathrm{const}+\tfrac12 d_f\,\delta_\nu\cos2(\theta+\psi_0)$, with the
  same $\psi_0$ and the same stationary set $\theta+\psi_0\in(\pi/2)\mathbb Z$; Thms.~single and
  nogo apply to every such $Q$. This trace identity is exact at $3\times3$.
  \emph{Order flip} ($d_f<0$): the frame is unchanged and the eigenvalue order is swapped, so
  $\psi_0\to\psi_0+\pi/2$, maxima and minima exchange, and $\theta_{23}\to90^\circ-\theta_{23}$
  as a label. \emph{Degenerate} ($d_f=0$, e.g.\ $n_\tau=-n_\mu \bmod N$ for a cosine label):
  $f(\He)|_E\propto\one$, every functional is flat in $\theta$, $Q$ is inert, and (a) fails.
\item \emph{The $3\times3$ commutator norm is not frozen.}
  $\Fnorm{[f(\He),\Hnu(\theta)]}^2=\sum_{i\neq j}(f_i-f_j)^2|(\Hnu)_{ij}|^2$. Its $E$--$E$ part is
  the paper's $C\sin^22(\theta+\psi_0)$ with $\delta_e\to d_f$. Its e-row part carries a second
  harmonic with coefficient $\tfrac12(f_\tau-f_\mu)(2f_e-f_\mu-f_\tau)(|w_\mu|^2-|w_\tau|^2)$,
  whose phase is set by the e-row of $\Hnu$, not by $\psi_0$. It vanishes only if that e-row is
  zero, $f_\mu=f_\tau$, or $f_e=\tfrac12(f_\mu+f_\tau)$.
\item \emph{$E$ tilted to the data value} ($E=\nu_1^\perp$). In the $(\nu_2,\nu_3)$ frame the
  compression has off-diagonal element $(f_\mu-f_e)(A+rB)$, with
  $A=\overline{U_{\mu2}}U_{\mu3}$, $B=\overline{U_{\tau2}}U_{\tau3}$ and
  $r=(f_\tau-f_e)/(f_\mu-f_e)$. The labels enter only through $r$; the family is a one-parameter
  curve of frames. For $\delta=0$ the frame aligns with the $\varepsilon$-term exactly at
  \[
    r^*=\frac{s_{23}(c_{12}c_{23}-s_{12}s_{23}s_{13})}{c_{23}(c_{12}s_{23}+s_{12}c_{23}s_{13})}.
  \]
  At $\theta_{13}=0$ this gives $r^*=1$, i.e.\ $f_\mu=f_\tau$: the only aligning label is the inert
  one. The mechanism's entire leverage is $\theta_{13}\neq0$. For $\delta\neq0,\pi$ the frame is
  complex and exact alignment is generically impossible, since
  $\min_r|A+rB|=|A|\,|\sin(\arg A-\arg B)|>0$. This is consistent with the audit's complex-case
  result. $Q$ acts only through $\nu_1$'s $\mu/\tau$ components, and alignment is one real equation
  in $(r,\theta_{23})$: a one-parameter fit to one target, which reparametrises $\theta_{23}$ and
  does not determine it.
\end{itemize}
\begin{proposition}[The aligning ratio is a unitarity identity]\label{prop:rstar}
For real $U$ ($\delta\in\{0,\pi\}$), the label ratio that aligns $Q_E$ with the $\nu_2$--$\nu_3$
frame is
\[
  r^*=-\frac{U_{\mu2}U_{\mu3}}{U_{\tau2}U_{\tau3}}
     =1+\frac{U_{e2}U_{e3}}{U_{\tau2}U_{\tau3}}
     \simeq1\mp\frac{2\tan\theta_{12}\sin\theta_{13}}{\sin2\theta_{23}}
     \quad(\delta=0,\pi).
\]
\end{proposition}
\noindent\emph{Proof.} Orthogonality of columns 2 and 3,
$\sum_\alpha U_{\alpha2}U_{\alpha3}=0$, gives $-U_{\mu2}U_{\mu3}=U_{e2}U_{e3}+U_{\tau2}U_{\tau3}$.
For the leading order, use $U_{e2}U_{e3}=s_{12}c_{13}s_{13}e^{-i\delta}$ and
$U_{\tau2}U_{\tau3}=-c_{12}s_{23}c_{23}c_{13}+O(s_{13})$. $\square$\\
So $r^*=1$ at $\theta_{13}=0$ is the statement $U_{e3}=0$, and the deviation of $r^*$ from 1 is
entirely the $U_{e3}$ term. Leading order gives $\approx0.80$ / $1.20$. The exact form reproduces the
lab's PART~2 values in all eight variant$\times\delta$ cells, e.g.\ NO IC19 (without SK-atm)
$0.8170349147$ at $\delta=0$ and $1.2179887361$ at $\delta=\pi$
(\texttt{analysis/d3\_rstar\_identity.py}, run from the verifier's D3 section). In the
reading B below, the octant rule is the subleading $U_{e3}$ term.\\
\emph{Known context.} A flavor-diagonal label operator is the residual $\mathbb Z_N$ generator $T$ of
the charged-lepton sector in discrete flavor models. There it fixes only the charged-lepton basis,
and the mixing comes from the neutrino-side symmetry: $U=U_l^\dagger U_\nu$, with $U_l$
diagonalising $T$ and $U_\nu$ the Klein-group generators~\cite{residual2014} (stated there; the
general setting is reviewed in~\cite{kingluhn2013,feruglioromanino2021}). Integer flavor charges are Froggatt--Nielsen charges, which set
hierarchies, not angles~\cite{froggattnielsen1979}. D3 is a consistency check against these known
results, not a new mechanism.\\
\begin{proposition}[The CP-odd channel is closed]\label{prop:jarlskog}
$\det[\hat\Phi,\Hnu]=2iJ\,(n_\mu-n_e)(n_\tau-n_\mu)(n_\tau-n_e)\,
(m_2^2-m_1^2)(m_3^2-m_2^2)(m_3^2-m_1^2)$, with
$J=\operatorname{Im}(U_{e1}U_{\mu2}U_{e2}^*U_{\mu1}^*)$ in the PDG sign convention
(Jarlskog~\cite{jarlskog1985}). The overall sign is convention-dependent (commutator order,
ordering of the differences, sign convention for $J$); the form here is verified numerically in the
lab's stated conventions (PDG $J$, $[\hat\Phi,\Hnu]$, differences as written; lab run in ef716dd). Labels therefore enter the CP-odd invariant only through a
Vandermonde factor that rescales $J$; they cannot orient anything through it.
\end{proposition}
\noindent The lab verified it exactly as quoted, with no sign or factor correction: the ratio of
the determinant to the formula is 1 to $10^{-11}$ at the four data points and on 2000 random points
(lab ef716dd, \texttt{run-2026-10-23-t3-jarlskog.txt}).\\
\emph{Setup.} Labels range over every assignment with $N=2,\dots,60$, $n_\mu\neq n_\tau$ and
residues $0..N-1$ (71\,980 tuples; pooling the sub-cycle sectors $V_m$ adds no new frames). Three
label forms are scanned: linear $\hat\Phi$, and the cosine (C) and sine (S) Hermitian parts of
$W=e^{2\pi i\hat\Phi/N}$. Scoring is on the real frame, $\delta\in\{0,180^\circ\}$. Two readings:
\emph{A}, with $E$ at its data value and the prediction being the frame implied by $Q_E$'s
eigenframe; \emph{B}, with $\theta_{23}$ solved from the alignment condition itself, so that data
$\theta_{23}$ is not an input. The $\delta=0$ and $\delta=180^\circ$ columns are near-mirrors
($\mu\leftrightarrow\tau$ sends $r\to1/r$), so the 8 variant$\times\delta$ cells are about 4
independent ones. Inverted-ordering cells are scored at $\delta\in\{0,180^\circ\}$, far from the
inverted-ordering data $\delta$ ($285^\circ$/$274^\circ$). The gate passes (PART 0--1): the closed-form $r^*$ returns the data
angles exactly, $r^*=1$ for every $\theta_{23}$ at $\theta_{13}=0$, and Lagrange interpolation is
exact.\\

\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Alignment ratio.} $r^*_{23}=0.816$--$0.820$ at $\delta=0$ and $1.218$--$1.222$ at

\item \emph{Not a distinguished rational.} $r^*$ does not sit at a small-denominator rational.
  Its smallest denominator in the window is 9--11 (reading B) and 3--5 (reading A). Against a
