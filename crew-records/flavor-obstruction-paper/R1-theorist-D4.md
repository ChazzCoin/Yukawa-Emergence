R1-theorist-D4 (item 11). Derived on paper, nothing run. Sources read: constructive_section.tex 17-50 and 197-330; theta23_obstruction_paper.tex 147-346.

SETUP. n>=3. Spectral form H_nu = sum_{k not in E} l_k P_k + lam P_E + (d/2) S, where E = 2-dim eigenspace (ν2,ν3), S = sigma_z in the (ν2,ν3) basis, d = l_2 - l_3 (d=0 at eps=0). R(t) rotates the (ν2,ν3) basis; H_nu(t) = R^T H_nu R = A + (d/2) S(t), A = sum l_k P_k + lam P_E, S(t) = cos2t sz - sin2t sx (checked by hand). Key fact used throughout: A acts as the scalar lam on E, so H_nu(t) depends on t only through S(t).

(a) AGREE, exact for any n. Any Q built only from the mass projectors outside E lies in the commutative algebra generated by {P_k, k not in E} and 1: Q = q0*1 + sum q_k P_k, since P_k P_j = delta_kj P_k. Then P_E Q P_E = q0 P_E, because P_k P_E = 0. q0 = 0 for Q4 = P_4 alone, and for any function g of H_nu, q0 = g(lam). The statement holds even at eps != 0: P_4 and P_1 are eigenprojectors of the Hermitian H_nu, so they are orthogonal to E = span(ν2,ν3) exactly. The compression is scalar at every eps. At eps != 0, g(H_nu)|_E = g(lam) + g-prime(lam)*(d/2)S + ..., a function of K|_E, so it shares K eigenframe and still cannot orient anything beyond K (same as the Remark on labels on mass states: Q = q(H_nu) commutes with H_nu, inert). Caveat to state: E must be the FULL lam-eigenspace. If a non-E state were degenerate with lam, E grows and the group becomes U(3) not U(2). In 3+1 Dm41 ~ 1 eV^2 excludes this. Statement for D4: "Any operator in the algebra generated by the spectral projectors of H_nu outside E acts on E as a multiple of the identity (P_E Q P_E = q0 P_E), for every n and every eps; it is inert on E and fails (a)."

(b) AGREE, with one correction to wording. N -> N*diag(1,R,1) is a right multiplication by a block that is the identity on columns 1 and 4, so columns 1 and 4 are exactly unchanged, and columns 2,3 mix: N_a3 -> -sin t N_a2 + cos t N_a3. So |N_mu3|, |N_tau3| change and, in general, so do theta12, theta13 and the active-block entries (cf. AUDIT ii; theta23 -> theta23 + t holds only when E is the mu-tau plane). The proof of Prop obstruction uses only H_nu|_E = lam*1_E, with no n dependence, so it carries over verbatim. Correction: at eps = 0 the individual |N_mu3|, |N_tau3| are NOT measurable, because vacuum oscillation with equal phases on ν2,ν3 depends only on the projectors P_1, P_4, P_E. They become observable only at eps != 0, and then through K eigenframe. So "measured |N_mu3| changes" should read "the label-level value of |N_mu3| changes". Invariants: P_E = N_E N_E^dagger (entries sum_{i in E} N_ai conj(N_bi)); the E-weights W_a = |N_a2|^2 + |N_a3|^2 (diagonal of P_E); P_1, P_4; |N_a1|, |N_a4| individually. Rows of N restricted to E, the 2-vectors (N_a2,N_a3), transform together by the right U(2) (SO(2) for real R); the Gram matrix of those rows is P_E, and it fixes them up to that U(2). So "rows of P_E" is right: P_E, not the individual rows. Statement for D4: "The rotation N -> N diag(1,R,1) fixes columns 1 and 4 and P_E, and changes the individual entries N_a2, N_a3; only P_E, P_1, P_4 are frame-independent." Note R may be complex U(2); the paper uses real SO(2).

(c) AGREE with a sharpening. Let h = P_E H_e P_E as a 2x2 in the (ν2,ν3) basis: h_ij = sum_a a_a conj(N_ai) N_aj, a_a = (m_e^2, m_mu^2, m_tau^2, x), D = h_22 - h_33. Then Tr(H_e H_nu(t)) = const + (d/2) Tr(h S(t)) = const + (d/2)[D cos2t - 2 Re(h_23) sin2t] = const + (d/2) Amp cos 2(t + psi0), with Amp = sqrt(D^2 + 4 (Re h_23)^2), psi0 = (1/2) atan2(2 Re h_23, D). Exact for any n and for E not invariant under H_e (E tilted out of the mu-tau plane, E with sterile weight); h is the compression. If d<0 (order of ν2,ν3 swapped), psi0 shifts by pi/2. The amplitude (d/2)*Amp reduces to the paper (1/2)delta_e delta_nu only when h is real and E invariant (Re h_23 = 0 in the H_e eigenframe, Amp = delta_e); Im h_23 drops out because R is real, which is the same point as the audit complex-case result (v). The same holds for Tr(H_e g(H_nu(t))) for any polynomial g, since H_nu(t)^2 = A^2 + d*lam*S + (d^2/4)P_E: still linear in S. Explicit x-dependence, with s_i = N_si, the sterile row on E, and h0 the active-only part: h_22 - h_33 = D0 + x(|s_2|^2 - |s_3|^2), h_23 = h0_23 + x conj(s_2) s_3, so psi0(x) = (1/2) atan2(2 Re h0_23 + 2x Re(conj(s_2) s_3), D0 + x(|s_2|^2 - |s_3|^2)) and Amp(x) from the same two numbers. x-independent iff s = 0 (E has no sterile component), or, more generally, iff the rank-1 matrix conj(s_i) s_j shares the eigenframe of h0, i.e. (s_2,s_3) is an eigenvector of h0 (then x changes only Amp). Otherwise x sweeps psi0 along a one-parameter curve of frames, the same structure as the label ratio r in Prop rstar. Statement for D4: "Tr(H_e(4) H_nu(t)) = const + (d/2) Amp(x) cos 2(t + psi0(x)), where psi0 and Amp depend on x only through the sterile components (N_s2, N_s3) of E; if these vanish the trace is the 3x3 single cosine."

Scope limit for (c) and the no-go (must be stated in D4): Thm single and Cor aligned are theorems about functionals of the COMPRESSIONS to E, exact in any n. They do NOT cover full-space functionals. In n>=3 the full ring is larger: Tr(H_e S H_e S) is quadratic in S, so it carries cos4(...) with a phase set by the E-to-complement couplings of H_e (P_E H_e P_perp), not by psi0. The Frobenius norm of the full commutator is not frozen. This is the D3 e-row result (second harmonic, 3x3) and AUDIT (i), and it carries to n=4 with more terms (the sterile row and column of H_e enter). So "the no-go does not depend on the flavor-space dimension" is TRUE for the compression-class (Thm single/monotone, E-E part of the commutator norm) and OVERSTATED for the full-space commutator norm or any functional using P_E H_e P_perp. D4 must carry the same restriction the D3 text has.

(d) AGREE with a narrowing. Exact content: (1) orthogonal dimension. If the added flavor dimension is orthogonal to E (N_s2 = N_s3 = 0, so E lies in the old space), H_e(4) = H_e(3) + x|s><s| leaves h unchanged, psi0, Amp are x-independent, and every compression-class functional is identical to the 3x3 one. Exact. (2) Mass-state operators outside E: (a). (3) Non-orthogonal case (3+1 with sterile content in E). The only change is the family psi0(x). But x is not defined by the theory or the mass/flavor data, so condition (b) fails (not externally oriented); x gives alignment for one chosen x, which reparametrises theta23 rather than determining it (same as D3 r). It passes (a),(c) only formally once x is chosen, and then (d) needs a null over x. So the conclusion is: orthogonal dimensions and mass-state operators cannot help; a sterile with E-content adds one free parameter. What it does NOT exclude: any Q whose compression to E is non-scalar and oriented by a datum independent of (H_e,H_nu). Non-unitary 3x3 block: this does not change the picture. A non-unitary active block of a unitary 4x4 N is just the same N with rows restricted, columns 2,3 still rotate by the same R, and P_E restricted to the active rows is still frame-invariant. The no-go is a statement about Hermitian operators on E, not about unitarity of N. But if non-unitarity is a separate Hermitian operator, e.g. eta ~ Y_nu M^-2 Y_nu^dagger in type-I seesaw (the standard form is N = (1-eta/2)U), then eta is NOT a function of (H_e,H_nu) in general, and eta|_E can be non-scalar with its own eigenframe. That is a genuinely new Q and would have to pass (a)-(e). Whether eta is in the theory class is the gap Q3 below. I did not derive its E-compression; that needs the seesaw structure. Statement for D4: "Adding flavor dimensions orthogonal to E, or operators built from mass states outside E, leaves the compression to E unchanged or scalar. A sterile state with components in E enters only through the undefined mass x, as a one-parameter family of frames. A useful Q needs a non-scalar action on E oriented by data outside (H_e,H_nu); the unitarity of N does not enter."

GAPS (questions for Chazz). Q1: does the theory define a sterile Yukawa / H_e on the sterile direction (what is x)? Without it x is free, as above. Q3: is a non-unitarity operator eta (seesaw) part of the relational class, or is the class limited to functions of (H_e,H_nu)? Q4: n=4 full-space commutator norm: I did not derive its harmonic content, only that the sterile row adds terms. Q5: the sizes of N_s2, N_s3 and of x are not given in this record, so how large the x-leverage is, is unknown to me.
