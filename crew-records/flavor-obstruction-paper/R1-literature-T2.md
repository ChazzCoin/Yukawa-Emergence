(2a) Branco-Rebelo-Silva-Marcos hep-ph/9810328: authors, title 'Degenerate and Quasi Degenerate Majorana Neutrinos', PRL 82 (1999) 683-686 CONFIRMED (arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/9810328). Abstract: 'We study mixing and CP violation of three left-handed Majorana neutrinos in the limit of exactly degenerate masses, identify the weak-basis invariant relevant for CP violation and show that the leptonic mixing matrix is parametrized only by two angles and one phase. After the lifting of the degeneracy, this parametrization accommodates the present data on atmospheric and solar neutrinos, as well as double beta decay. Some of the leptonic mixing ansatze suggested in the literature correspond to special cases of this parametrization.' The abstract says NOTHING on CP parity, and the paper is about THREE degenerate neutrinos, not a pair. Verdict: direction supported for 3 neutrinos in CP-conserving limit; pair-level phrasing not in this paper (2x2 sub-case, not shown there). Cite with those pages.
