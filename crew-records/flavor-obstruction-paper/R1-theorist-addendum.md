## B. Scope of Thm single / nogo: the paper's invariant list is complete only for 2×2
Thm single's proof (tex 278-286) uses: the ring of U(2)-invariants of a pair is generated by Tr X, Tr X², Tr Y, Tr Y², Tr XY (Cayley–Hamilton, 2×2). That is correct on E. For a pair of 3×3 Hermitians (Procesi/Sibirskii; R1-literature §) the generating set also contains Tr X²Y, Tr XY², Tr X²Y², and a degree-6 word (Tr X²Y²XY, which gives the CP-odd Tr([X,Y]³)); Tr X³, Tr Y³ too. Tr X²Y² and Tr XYXY are independent in 3×3, and ‖[X,Y]‖² = 2Tr X²Y² − 2Tr XYXY. Consequence:
- Thm single, Cor aligned, Thm monotone, Thm nogo are theorems about E-restricted (2×2) functionals; as written ("any relational functional of (H_e|_E, H_ν(θ)|_E)") they stand. They do not extend to relational functionals of the 3×3 pair; "every basis-independent penalty from the sector Hermitians" (tex 181-182, 435) is then an overreach.
- Compatible with audit (i): Tr X²Y², hence ‖[H_e,H_ν]‖², is the invariant that carries the second phase φ_u. The one-cosine statement for Tr(H_eH_ν) survives (u absent) and for the eigenframe functional; it fails (small) for Tr((H_eH_ν)²) and (larger) for the commutator.
- The CP-odd Tr([H_e,H_ν]³) is outside the paper's class. T0 finds it ≡ 0 at δ=0 and with range 1.9e-13 at δ=197° (README l.155), i.e. no usable θ-dependence in their setting. I did not verify that and don't know why it is that small; it is not a proof it cannot carry orientation.
Status of the extended statement: derived on paper (invariant theory is standard); the T0 numbers are theirs, not rechecked by me.

## C. "Minimiser = 90°" — confirm, with a correction to what it is
From §3(v): ‖[H_E,H_ν|_E]‖² = ½δe²δν²(1−r²cos²φ'). The minimiser in θ is cosφ'=±1 for any r > 0: same θ locations as the real case (the xz-projection aligned), so the location statement holds generally. But the value of the PMNS angle there is not 0/90°: the 2-3 overlap is |⟨v_e|v_ν⟩|² = (1 + n·m)/2 = (1 ± r)/2, so cos²θ₂₃ = (1±r)/2 (up to column rephasing). θ₂₃ = 0 or 90° only when r = 1 (n_y = 0, H_E real up to a phase); r→0 gives 45°. So "minimiser = 90° (or 0)" holds for real H_e (or r = 1), and in general is replaced by arccos√((1±r)/2) with 𝓔_min = ½δe²δν²(1−r²) > 0. T0's R1 minimisers 89.88–90.00° at δ = 197° are consistent with r ≈ 1 there (small n_y), and its R2b 88.38–89.28° with r slightly below 1; I did not compute r for their matrices. The "3×3 tilted H_e" caveat (audit i) additionally shifts the minimiser (62.24° vs 62.17°).
