\paragraph{Relation to known results.}
None of the ingredients is new as mathematics. What this paper adds is their assembly into a no-go for the finite spectral triple.
(i) A degenerate pair of neutrino masses leaves the rotation inside the pair unphysical. This is the pair analogue of the statement that for three exactly degenerate neutrinos the mixing matrix is defined only up to an orthogonal transformation~\cite{brsm1999}. For Majorana neutrinos the residual freedom is $\mathrm{O}(2)$ only at equal CP parity (\cite{brsm1999}; Sec.~\ref{sec:D2}). Proposition~\ref{prop:obstruction} restates it as a flat direction of the spectral action.
(ii) The generators used in Theorem~\ref{thm:single}, $\Tr X,\ \Tr X^2,\ \Tr Y,\ \Tr Y^2,\ \Tr XY$, are the classical minimal generating set of $2\times2$ matrix invariants of a pair~\cite{sibirskii1968,drensky2005}. The theorem adds only that conjugating one argument moves $\Tr XY$ alone. It concerns the compression to $E$. On $\mathbb C^3$ the invariant ring of the pair is larger: it contains, for example, $\Tr(\He^2\Hnu^2)$ and the CP-odd $\Tr([\He,\Hnu]^3)$.
(iii) Jarlskog introduced the commutator as the measure of joint diagonalizability and its determinant as the CP-odd invariant~\cite{jarlskog1985}. Proposition~\ref{prop:jarlskog} is his identity with the charged-lepton masses replaced by the labels.
(iv) Corollary~\ref{cor:external} is the residual-symmetry statement in operator form. A $\mathbb Z_2$ generator with a degenerate eigenvalue fixes one column of the mixing matrix and leaves the angle in the complementary plane arbitrary. A second generator, non-degenerate on that plane, is needed to fix it~\cite{lam2008,fonsecagrimus2014,king2017}. The value $\pi/4$ is the $\mu$--$\tau$-reflection prediction~\cite{harrison2002,fhz2013}. The joint-alignment condition of Sec.~\ref{sec:D3b}, that $\nu_2$ be an eigenvector of $Q$, is the same partial-pattern structure with the fixed column $\nu_2$.
We have not found the no-go stated in this form: every unitarily invariant functional of the intrinsic sector data on $E$ is stationary at alignment, and its stationary set does not depend on the masses. We present it as known facts assembled in spectral-triple language, not as new mathematics.
