\section{Beyond the relational class: other sources of orientation}\label{sec:r2}
%=======================================================================
The no-go and the battery of Sec.~\ref{sec:constructive} show that nothing built from $(\He,\Hnu)$,
and no added flavor dimension, orients the $\nu_2$--$\nu_3$ frame. This section tests sources that
are \emph{other data} or \emph{input-free}, together with the hypothesis that nothing needs
explaining. The criteria are (a)--(e) of Sec.~\ref{sec:conditions}, with (b) widened: oriented by
data other than $\theta_{23}$, \emph{or by no continuous input at all}. Every candidate is scored
on two questions: does it give near-maximal mixing, and which octant does it pick?

\subsection{What is established: near-maximal, not the octant}\label{sec:octant}
% From theorist-4 (crew folder files/R2-theorist.md); every number from analysis/data/nufit6_ranges.csv.
NuFIT~6.0~\cite{nufit6} gives, for normal ordering, $\theta_{23}=48.5^{+0.7}_{-0.9}{}^\circ$ (IC19,
without SK-atm) and $43.3^{+1.0}_{-0.8}{}^\circ$ (IC24, with SK-atm); for inverted ordering,
$48.6^{+0.7}_{-0.9}{}^\circ$ and $47.9^{+0.7}_{-0.9}{}^\circ$. The two data sets put the
normal-ordering best fit in opposite octants. In units of the side-appropriate $1\sigma$ error,
$45^\circ$ lies $3.9\sigma$ and $1.7\sigma$ (NO) and $4.0\sigma$ and $3.2\sigma$ (IO) from the best
fits. But the $\chi^2$ profile is not Gaussian, and every published $3\sigma$ interval
($[41.0,50.5]^\circ$ and $[41.3,49.9]^\circ$ NO; $[41.4,50.6]^\circ$ and $[41.5,49.8]^\circ$ IO)
contains $45^\circ$. What the data establish is that $\theta_{23}$ is near-maximal: the best fits lie
within $3.6^\circ$ of $45^\circ$, and the $3\sigma$ ranges within $[41.0^\circ,50.6^\circ]$. The octant,
and whether $\theta_{23}$ differs from $45^\circ$ at all, are not established. Each mechanism below
is therefore scored on two questions: does it give near-maximal $\theta_{23}$, and which octant does
it pick.

\subsection{D11: an extremum principle}\label{sec:D11}
\emph{Candidate.} Maximal CP violation:
$J=s_{12}c_{12}s_{13}c_{13}^2s_{23}c_{23}\sin\delta$ is extremal in $\theta_{23}$ at $45^\circ$ and
in $\delta$ at $\pm90^\circ$, so a maximal-$J$ principle gives the $\mu$--$\tau$-reflection pattern
without a symmetry.\\
% Proposition and proof from theorist-4 (files/R2-theorist.md), exact algebra.
\begin{proposition}[Maximal CP violation is octant-blind]\label{prop:Jextremum}
Let $J=A\sin2\theta_{23}\sin\delta$ with $A=\tfrac18\sin2\theta_{12}\sin2\theta_{13}\cos\theta_{13}>0$.
(i) On $\theta_{23}\in(0,\pi/2)$ the critical points of $J$ are exactly $(\pi/4,\pm\pi/2)$.
(ii) For every fixed $\delta$ with $\sin\delta\neq0$, $\partial_{\theta_{23}}J=0$ iff
$\theta_{23}=\pi/4$.
(iii) $J(\pi/2-\theta_{23},\delta)=J(\theta_{23},\delta)$ identically, so no function of $J$
distinguishes the octants, at any order.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
$\partial_{\theta_{23}}J=2A\cos2\theta_{23}\sin\delta$ and $\partial_\delta J=A\sin2\theta_{23}\cos\delta$.
On the open interval $\sin2\theta_{23}\neq0$, which gives (i) and (ii). (iii) holds because
$\sin2(\pi/2-\theta_{23})=\sin2\theta_{23}$.
\end{proof}
\noindent Near $(45^\circ,-90^\circ)$, with $x=\theta_{23}-45^\circ$ and $y=\delta+90^\circ$,
$J=-A\cos2x\cos y=-A+2Ax^2+\tfrac{A}{2}y^2+O(4)$, with no cross term. An offset in $\delta$
therefore induces no shift in $\theta_{23}$; only the width depends on $\delta$, through the
$\theta_{23}$-curvature $4A|\sin\delta|$. The same holds verbatim for the weak-basis CP-odd invariant
$\Tr([\He,\Hnu]^3)\propto J$. The $e$-row Majorana invariants $\operatorname{Im}(U_{e1}U_{e3}^*)$ and
$\operatorname{Im}(U_{e2}U_{e3}^*)$ do not depend on $\theta_{23}$ at all.\\
\emph{A CP-even exception (leading order, under test).} The misalignment norm is not octant-blind.
To leading order in $m_\mu^2/m_\tau^2$ and $\Delta m^2_{21}/\Delta m^2_{31}$,
$-\tfrac12\Tr([\He,\Hnu]^2)=m_\tau^4\,|U_{\tau3}|^2(1-|U_{\tau3}|^2)\,(\Delta m^2_{31})^2$, which is
maximal at $|U_{\tau3}|^2=\tfrac12$, i.e.\ $\theta_{23}^*\approx44.4^\circ$ at
$\sin^2\theta_{13}=0.022$ (first octant, independent of $\delta$ at this order). [pending: lab check
of the exact argmax, theorist-4's C2]\\

does the principle allow once $\delta$ is at its data value; and is any other natural invariant
